Scaffolding up at the Gory Manor House

12 August 2026 Nizhny Novgorod

The Manor House at Gory is now fully scaffolded. It is an unglamorous milestone, but a decisive one: until the roof and façade are sound, none of the interior work that will turn this building into a communal space, workshop and recording studio can responsibly begin.

A retreat is only as good as the room artists gather in at the end of the day.

What is happening on site

  • Roof timbers surveyed, with replacement sections milled locally.
  • Façade repointing and window reinstatement across the main elevation.
  • Outbuildings prepared for conversion into visitor accommodation.
  • First-fix routing planned for the recording studio's acoustic isolation.

The site sits roughly equidistant between Moscow and St. Petersburg, deliberately remote, and is intended to give Russian and international artists access to retreat and creative facilities otherwise closed to them. The facility remains on course to open in mid-2027.
