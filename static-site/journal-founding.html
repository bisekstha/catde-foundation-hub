Why we founded CATDE

18 June 2026 Foundation

The Foundation was established in June 2026 to fund worthwhile artistic projects that also carry a clear societal benefit. That second half of the sentence does a lot of work: it is the reason some proposals are approved and others, however elegant, are not.

We approve every project ourselves, and we attend the end results.

How we work

  • Chris and Tanya personally review and approve each project.
  • Funding follows practical milestones rather than announcements.
  • Projects are visited, and their openings attended.

Approved work currently spans a literary café and events venue in Colombo, school literature donations in Nuwara Eliya, the restoration of a Hindu temple, a twenty-room artistic retreat in Nizhny Novgorod, an opera in St. Petersburg, a liturgy staged in Belgrade, summer camp horse riding for students in Shanghai, and university fees in France. A ballet school in Sri Lanka is among the projects in the pipeline.
