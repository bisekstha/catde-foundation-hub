Our purpose

Funding art that returns something lasting

Founded by Chris and Tanya Devonshire-Ellis in June 2026, the Foundation provides funding to worthwhile artistic projects across continents — from a literary house in Sri Lanka to an artists' retreat deep in the Russian countryside.

Chris and Tanya personally oversee projects, approve them and attend the end results. Nothing is delegated to a committee — every commitment is made in person, and every outcome is seen through.

Nizhny Novgorod, Russia

The Gory Artistic Retreat

This substantial project has involved the acquisition of numerous buildings in a remote area of Russia equidistant between Moscow and St. Petersburg and is designed to allow Russian and other artists access to retreat and creative facilities denied them due to EU border restrictions.

Opening September 2026

Nugegoda, Sri Lanka

The Jam Fruit Tree Literary House

Jam Fruit Publications are Sri Lanka's primary publisher of local literary talent and needed a new location to expand their business as well as offer literary events and assistance to local writers, artists and musicians.
