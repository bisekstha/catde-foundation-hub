Opening mid-2027

Nizhny Novgorod, Russia

The Gory Artistic Retreat

This substantial project has involved the acquisition of numerous buildings in a remote area of Russia equidistant between Moscow and St. Petersburg and is designed to allow Russian and other artists access to retreat and creative facilities denied them due to EU border restrictions. Numerous buildings are being restored and upgraded for visitor accommodation and use, with the main Manor House to provide a communal space and workshop as well as a recording studio. The facility is expected to open in mid-2027.

Opening mid-2027

Nugegoda, Sri Lanka

The Jam Fruit Tree Literary House

Jam Fruit Publications are Sri Lanka's primary publisher of local literary talent and needed a new location to expand their business as well as offer literary events and assistance to local writers, artists and musicians. Their new facility will open in September 2026.
