Journal — Nugegoda, Sri Lanka

A literary house takes shape in Nugegoda

28 July 2026 Nugegoda, Sri Lanka

Jam Fruit Publications is Sri Lanka's primary publisher of local literary talent, and had outgrown its premises. The new literary house gives the imprint room to expand its publishing programme while opening its doors to the writers, artists and musicians who make up its community.

More than a bookshop

The ground floor holds the reading room and a small stage for launches, readings and music evenings. Behind it sits a support desk where local writers can get practical help — manuscript advice, editing referrals and printing guidance — at no cost.

  • Reading room and retail space for Sri Lankan literature.
  • Events programme of launches, readings and recitals.
  • Free advisory service for emerging local writers.

The facility opens in September 2026.
