The Gory Artistic Retreat

Nizhny Novgorod, Russia Opening mid-2027

This substantial project has involved the acquisition of numerous buildings in a remote area of Russia equidistant between Moscow and St. Petersburg, and is designed to allow Russian and other artists access to retreat and creative facilities denied them due to EU border restrictions.

The buildings

Numerous buildings are being restored and upgraded for visitor accommodation and use, with the main Manor House to provide a communal space and workshop as well as a recording studio.

  • Twenty rooms of visitor accommodation across restored village buildings
  • Communal workshop and rehearsal space in the Manor House
  • Recording studio for resident musicians and composers
Somewhere quiet enough to work, and open to those who cannot travel further.
